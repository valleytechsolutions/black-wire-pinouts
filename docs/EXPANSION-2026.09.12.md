# Wiring & protocols — collection 2026.09.12

Fifteen original Black Wire connection guides and SVG diagrams cover Ethernet and telephone connectors, UART, RS-232, RS-485, CAN, I2C, SPI, SWD, JTAG and RFID/NFC roles. Module examples distinguish ELECHOUSE ST25R3916B SPI from I2C, six-pin PN7160 MINI V1 from eight-pin variants, and PN532 V3 host-mode switches from other revisions.

[Browse the guides](../WIRING.md) · [Source evidence](../catalog/wiring-sources.json) · [Diagram attribution and hashes](../catalog/wiring-attributions.json)

These diagrams explain connections and are **not additional physical board pinouts**. Existing board/module counts, source images and Power Desk profiles are unchanged. Original writing and artwork are CC BY 4.0, by Kal / Valleytech Solutions; cited manufacturer documents retain their own rights. No source artwork has been relabeled as original Black Wire artwork.

The guides provide scope, use cases, connection tables, signal direction, preparation checks and exact module crosslinks. Manufacturer documents were inspected; no independent electrical/bench certification is claimed. The ST25R3916B module source is explicitly a V0.3 Draft. PN532 V3 legacy voltage wording is not imported as an approved limit. Telephone and CAN guidance does not supply a universal automotive or country-specific harness map.

The app and public/static GitHub wiki use the same catalog content. The physical book draft and existing manufacturer collections are not edited. Editorial edition: **First Edition / 2026**.

Walnut Pi and the Pico-family gaps identified in the preceding coverage audit remain open; this wiring update does not claim to fill them.
