# November 25 hardware and GPIO references

First Edition / 2026 · Digital collection 2026.11.2

23 new boards and devices, 17 new maker modules and 2 earlier XIAO records with revised pad notes. The collection adds 28 distinct original physical-pinout images and two printable PDFs. Manufacturer links open 11 additional physical diagrams and 3 peripheral-signal references.

## New hardware and revised diagrams

The Arduino Nano XL Link (ABX00182) and Connector Carrier (ASX00090) have separate printable pinouts. Arduino announced them on November 10. On the Seeed XIAO nRF54L15 and Sense, the rear V1.2 silkscreen identifies the revised 3V3, RST2, SWCLK2 and SWDIO2 pad positions. Seeed publishes one rear diagram for both V1.2 boards. The earlier listings identify the old layout.

LILYGO published T-Display CM0 and T-CAN485 C5 storefront listings on November 16 and 17. Their connector maps open on the manufacturer’s site. The T-Beam 1W2 and T5 e-Paper Basic drawings cover onboard signals. DFRobot’s November 12 SEN0661 release and M5Stack’s November 13 U233 release include diagrams for their external connectors.

Flipper One is under development. Soldered lists the NULA Node RP2040 as coming soon. TI’s November LaunchPad manual dates the document. An exact LP-EM-CC2341R10 retail launch date remains unverified. Older Olimex, Arduino Modulino and Embeetle maps fill gaps in the collection.
