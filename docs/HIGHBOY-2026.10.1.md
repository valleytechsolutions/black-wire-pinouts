The earlier **REV 2.2 MVP / ESP32-S3** connector-label image remains a separate historical record. Its type is corrected to **board labeling image**: supporting labels do not establish a complete physical pinout. Pin assignments must not be transferred between these revisions.

The updated collection contains 3,643 board reference entries, 2,011 records with files, and 1,574 physical pinout-image entries. The physical count falls by one because of the corrected historical classification; this schematic does not add a physical pinout.
