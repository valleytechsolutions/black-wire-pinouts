## Revision details

- HiiBot IoTs2 v1 assigns SPI clock to IO40 and the programmable button to IO21. On v2 those functions move to IO39 and IO0. Choose the drawing for the board marking.
- The two PicoADK drawings describe the 48-pin RP2040 audio board. The separate PicoADK v2 uses RP2350 hardware.
- The Stamp diagrams apply to the RP2040 Stamp configuration. The rectangular and Round Carrier have different header layouts.
- The ATtiny1616 maker documents rotated rear labeling on revisions A/B. Compare the physical PCB with the front-view diagram.
- The PicoPlanet rear-pad drawing prints the clock signal as “SLC”. Its lettering is preserved.

## Drawings held for clarification

- **ACH-ESP32-S3F16R8**. The color diagram assigns native USB D−/D+ to GPIO18/17. The board schematic assigns D−/D+ to GPIO19/20. [Board schematic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AchirawareElectronics/ACH-ESP32-S3-F16R8/01bc4b23aa8330f6afeb70c48b6dde79484896aa/hardware/schematic.pdf) · [Espressif USB reference](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/schematic-checklist.html#usb)
- **RP2350B BellsNWhistles**. The pinout repeats position 6 at the eighth pad of the left header. The maker schematic and PCB identify J8 pin 6 as GPIO4 and J8 pin 8 as ground. [Board schematic](https://github.com/riktw/RP2350B_BellsNWhistles/blob/55bc32ba00a311331ed667b862f2307e5e4073cf/rp2350_BellsnWhistles.pdf) · [PCB layout](https://github.com/riktw/RP2350B_BellsNWhistles/blob/55bc32ba00a311331ed667b862f2307e5e4073cf/rp2350_BellsnWhistles.kicad_pcb)
